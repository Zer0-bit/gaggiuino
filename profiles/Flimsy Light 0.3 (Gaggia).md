Turbo profile for washed and clean natural, light and ultra-light roasted coffees. 

Designed to bring out sweetness and fruity acidity in these coffees while minimising muddying roasty, straw-like, unpleasant, and "brown" generic coffee flavours often present when trying to push extraction with contact time and temperature. It works off the theory that extraction yield is not always even across the solubles in coffee, and that we can adjust what solubles we are trying to extract more or less of by tweaking the various levers of extraction.

A culmination of learnings and experience across turbo-style profiles (extdos, LC, LHL) and being chronically online reading articles and watching videos about espresso.
